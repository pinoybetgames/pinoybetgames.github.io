MANILA, PHILIPPINES – September 3, 2026 – Play Beyond Games (PBG), a leading developer of culturally resonant HTML5 casino games, today announced a strategic partnership with X9-Gaming (x9asia.com), a premier online gaming and entertainment platform. This collaboration will bring PBG’s complete portfolio gaming titles to X9-Gaming’s rapidly growing player base.
Under the new agreement, X9-Gaming players will gain full access to Play Beyond Games’ highly engaging catalog, which includes fan-favorite titles such as Goodluck Money, Jeepney Scatter, Boracay Beauties, and the latest addition Royal Ace. Built specifically for fast, frictionless integration and high player retention, PBG’s lightweight games are designed to perform seamlessly across all devices, delivering an optimized experience for X9-Gaming’s community.
"We are incredibly excited to partner with X9-Gaming, a platform that shares our commitment to delivering exceptional and accessible player experiences," said Bert C., CEO of Play Beyond Games. "Our mission has always been to build games that operators can integrate quickly and players can enjoy genuinely. By bringing our localized, high-RTP titles to x9asia.com, we are taking a significant step forward in our goal to elevate culturally relevant iGaming content across the region."
The integration leverages PBG’s clean API architecture, ensuring that the rollout is smooth and that the games load quickly without degrading the player experience. For X9-Gaming, the addition of PBG's portfolio reinforces their commitment to offering a diverse, player-first environment with unique, high-performing content that resonates with local and regional audiences.
"Partnering with Play Beyond Games aligns perfectly with our strategy to offer fresh, localized, and highly engaging content to our users," a spokesperson for X9-Gaming stated . "The cultural authenticity and proven mechanics of PBG’s titles make them a fantastic addition to our platform, and we are confident they will be a massive hit with our community."
The integration is officially live, and X9-Gaming players can now experience the full suite of Play Beyond Games titles directly on x9vip.org.
